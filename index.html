About

My research lies at the intersection of operating systems, computer architecture, and networking, with a focus on datacenter systems. I explore how hardware/software co-design can reduce underutilization of shared hardware resources, improve data movement, and preserve safety. Recently, I have been applying some of the same principles to systems for LLM inference.

Outside of research, I enjoy badminton, soccer, and FPS games. I come from Lucknow, India, a UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy.
