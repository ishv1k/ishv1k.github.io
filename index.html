Research

I study how operating systems and hardware can work together to build efficient, safe systems. My work spans storage congestion control and IOMMU-based memory protection in virtualized environments, including formalizing the trade-offs between safety and performance in I/O protection.

I also explore systems for machine learning, focusing on GPU memory pooling and LLM serving through NVLink peer-to-peer access.
